# Temple economy

Temple prices are balanced around time-to-afford rather than the old 5–120 coin catalogue. A platform coin is worth 40 coins and globally respawns after 90 seconds. With 13 shared platforms, the theoretical room-wide income is about 347 coins per minute; split among four active fighters it is about 87 coins per player-minute before contested ground loot and combat rewards.

| Tier | Typical price | Intended time at shared platform income |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | 240–520 | 3–6 minutes |
| Established | 620–1,100 | 7–13 minutes |
| Rare | 1,450–2,000 | 17–23 minutes |
| Epic | 3,300–4,400 | 38–50 minutes |
| Relic | 7,600+ | long-term goal |

Coin sinks include shop purchases, build placement, fusion fees, and the 20% PvP death drop. Resale returns 25%, preventing risk-free flipping. Fishing provides modest active income. Monsters pay 280–520 coins and equipment but are limited to one active timed monster.

Ground loot is server generated every 28–52 seconds, capped at ten active drops, expires after five minutes, and uses unique IDs. Coin, material, weapon and armor rolls are weighted. Duplicate materials remain separate inventory units and are valid fusion catalysts, so every pickup keeps economic value. Pickup is atomic and idempotent; a drop is removed before the updated world is broadcast, so simultaneous clients cannot duplicate it. Spectators cannot pick up, buy, build, fight or fish.
